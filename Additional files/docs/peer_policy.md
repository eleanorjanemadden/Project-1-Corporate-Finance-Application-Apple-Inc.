# Apple peer policy — recorded before calculating multiples, 2026-10-19
Use large profitable US GAAP platform businesses with recurring ecosystem monetization, global exposure, strong balance sheets and material technology investment. Microsoft and Alphabet are selected as imperfect platform cross-checks; neither is a pure hardware peer. Microsoft has more enterprise subscriptions/cloud and Alphabet more advertising; both have different capex intensity and regulatory risk. Exclude auto dealers (Lab 7), banks, unprofitable AI firms and transaction multiples. Samsung hardware is relevant but conglomerate mix, IFRS, currency and fiscal alignment require a separate reconciliation; exclude from this calculation.

Use one common quote date, unadjusted close, USD millions, GAAP trailing EBIT and net income for identical 12-month windows where possible. EV = equity market value + interest-bearing debt - cash/securities (cash convention shown separately); P/E = market equity / GAAP net income. Include all Alphabet share classes if admitted. Do not mix adjusted EPS with GAAP EBIT. Operating leases stay operating, with rent in EBIT; no lease liability subtraction without capitalizing earnings. Debt book value is an explicit proxy. Fiscal ends and share count dates must be shown. Reject stale/inconsistent observations before using a median. Report unavailable data rather than fabricate a peer value. No automatic averaging with DCF and no control/synergy transactions.

Annual-base fallback: a retrospective historical multiple can be shown as a diagnostic only. It cannot establish an October 2026 purchase recommendation. Report limitations for any fiscal-end lag.
